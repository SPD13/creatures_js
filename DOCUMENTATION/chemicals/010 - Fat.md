# 010 - Fat

Fat is the creature's **dietary fat input chemical** — the raw, un-digested lipid that enters the body from food and nothing else. It is the single gateway by which fat-rich foods (cheese, meat, nuts, fat pills, fatty seeds, and anything else whose CAOS script writes `STIM WRIT TARG 78 …`) deliver lipid energy into the biochemistry. Unlike the three active fat-storage tiers (Fatty Acid, Triglyceride, Adipose Tissue), Fat is not a metabolic intermediate and is not involved in any energy-release reaction: its role begins and ends with **digestion**. One unit of Fat is cleaved by reaction 3 into three units of Triglyceride plus one unit of Cholesterol, and that is the only thing the body ever does with it.

Fat has **no initial concentration at birth** (a newborn starts with zero Fat in its bloodstream), **no organ emitter**, **no receptors**, **no drive or brain coupling**, and an effectively **infinite passive half-life** (≈9.07 × 10¹⁰ ticks, decay rate 1.0). It is therefore a pure "flow-through" chemical — food injects it, reaction 3 consumes it, and nothing else touches it. The creature cannot sense its own Fat level directly; once the digestive reaction has run, Fat becomes indistinguishable from any other Triglyceride or Cholesterol produced elsewhere in the body, and the rest of the fat pipeline takes over.

This narrow, single-purpose design makes Fat the **conceptual dividing line** between the world's food system (which speaks in raw "Fat", "Protein", "Carbohydrate" terms via stims 78–82) and the creature's internal biochemistry (which works in Triglyceride, Fatty Acid, Adipose, Cholesterol, Glucose, etc.). Reaction 3 is the enzymatic border crossing: everything upstream of it is "food in the stomach", everything downstream is "body chemistry".

In the stock C3 bootstrap, stim 78 is written by fatty food items such as apples (trace), fungi, and other edibles defined in `Assets/Bootstrap/001 World/*.cos`; custom agents (toys, injectors, drugged foods, metaroom feeders) are also free to write to chem 10 directly via `CHEM 10 <amount>` if they wish to simulate fat ingestion bypassing the stim pipeline.

Fat has **no receptors at all** — no drive receptor, no brain receptor, no reaction-modulating receptor, no muscle or neuron target. The creature cannot feel "how much Fat is in the blood" directly; the only way Fat influences behaviour or physiology is indirectly, through the Triglyceride and Cholesterol that reaction 3 produces. Those downstream chemicals carry the actual signalling (satiety via Triglyceride receptors 26/38/48, structural pool via Cholesterol, etc.).

## Role in Game Mechanics

### The border between food and body chemistry

Creatures 3 models food in a two-layer system. The outer layer is the **stim layer**, where edible agents carry seven "nutrition" stims (roughly 78 = Fat, 79 = Protein, 80 = Carbohydrate, and related) that are written on `eat` actions. The inner layer is the **chemical layer**, where the creature's own body runs 140+ named metabolites through dozens of reactions. Fat is one of only a handful of chemicals whose job is to **bridge these two layers** — its stim-layer name and its chemical-layer name refer to the same thing, unlike (for example) Glucose [3], which is a purely internal metabolite, or Hunger for fat [146], which is a purely behavioural drive.

When a Norn eats a piece of cheese:

```
  Cheese agent's eat script
          │
          │   STIM WRIT TARG 78 <dose>
          ▼
  Stim → biochemistry mapping
          │
          │   stim 78 writes to chem 10
          ▼
  Fat [10] concentration rises
          │
          │   reaction 3 (HL ~116, gene 25)
          ▼
  Triglyceride [8] + Cholesterol [7]     ← from here the normal fat pipeline runs
          │
          ▼
  Adipose (9), Fatty Acid (6), etc.
```

Reaction 3 is therefore the **digestive checkpoint**: until it runs, the fat a creature just swallowed is biochemically inert; the moment it runs, the fat has been absorbed and the body can treat it like any other lipid.

### Why Fat has a half-life in reaction 3 but not in decay

Reaction 3 runs at HL ~116 ticks (Medium speed). That means a single meal's Fat bolus is roughly 50 % digested after ~12 seconds of real time (at 10 Hz) and essentially fully digested after ~60 seconds. This rate is fast enough that a creature's blood doesn't stay "full of raw Fat" for long (food gets absorbed in roughly a minute of in-game time), but slow enough that overeating in one sitting does produce a measurable, temporary Fat spike — and therefore a temporary Triglyceride/Cholesterol spike downstream, which in turn drives the satiety receptors (receptor 38 on Triglyceride at locus 0 of the Reaction organ's Somatic tissue) to say "I'm full of fat" for a while. The asymmetric design — **infinite passive persistence plus a fast enzymatic consumer** — guarantees that no molecule of ingested fat is ever "lost" but that the absorption process still produces realistic meal-timing effects.

Contrast this with Adipose (9), the only core metabolite with real passive decay: Adipose is designed to leak over time precisely *because* it represents long-term storage that must not accumulate forever. Fat is not storage at all — it's a transient input — so giving it a passive decay would be nonsensical: a swallowed meal shouldn't evaporate on its own.

### The 3:1 Fat-to-Triglyceride stoichiometry

Reaction 3 produces **three Triglycerides per Fat**, which reflects the real-world chemistry of dietary triglycerides (each is physically three fatty-acid chains bound to a glycerol backbone). Because downstream reaction 15 further cleaves each Triglyceride into 3 Fatty Acids, the amplification chain for ingested fat is:

```
  1 Fat (stim 78)
    ── reaction 3 (HL 116) ──►  3 Triglyceride + 1 Cholesterol
                                      │
                                      │ reaction 15 (HL 47) on each TG
                                      ▼
                                9 Fatty Acid
                                      │
                                      │ reaction 17 (HL 52) on each FA
                                      ▼
                         72 Pyruvate + 54 ATP   (full β-oxidation yield)
```

So a single unit of ingested Fat, fully oxidised, yields ~72 Pyruvate and ~54 ATP — a very high energy return. This is why fat-rich foods are the **calorically densest** way to feed a Norn: compare 1 Fat ⇒ ~54 ATP with 1 Carbohydrate ⇒ ~8 ATP (rough order of magnitude through the glycolysis + TCA chain). High-fat food items (cheese, meat, fat pills) are therefore the canonical way to rapidly raise a starving Norn's Adipose reserves.

### The Cholesterol co-product

Reaction 3 is also the **primary source of Cholesterol** in the body — every meal that contains Fat also loads the creature with Cholesterol at a 1 : 1 stoichiometry. Cholesterol is a structural/hormonal lipid that feeds into other downstream branches (Vitamin B synthesis, membrane maintenance in the Reproductive organ, etc.), and its tie to dietary Fat means that **a creature on a low-fat diet will also run low on Cholesterol**. This gives the genome an implicit channel for "fat-starvation has side effects beyond just low Adipose": the co-produced Cholesterol pool is a silent second signal that depends on recent dietary fat intake.

In terms of the overall lipid economy:

- Fat drives Triglyceride **up** (three per unit).
- Fat drives Cholesterol **up** (one per unit).
- Neither Triglyceride nor Cholesterol feeds back into Fat — the reaction is strictly one-way.

### Why there is no Fat receptor

It might seem odd that the creature has no sensor for its own dietary-fat concentration given how many receptors monitor everything else (Glucose at locus 2, Triglyceride at locus 0, Amino Acid at locus 2 of Circulatory, etc.). The design reason is that **Fat is transient**: because reaction 3 consumes it quickly (HL 116), any given Fat molecule only exists in the blood for ~1–2 minutes. A receptor tuned to Fat would therefore report "I just ate" rather than "I am well-fed" — which is not a useful long-term signal. Instead, the biochemistry reads satiety from the **downstream Triglyceride pool**, which is much longer-lived (infinite half-life) and therefore a better integrator of "how fat-replete is this creature right now". This is why receptors 26, 38 and 48 (carb-hunger shuffle, fat-hunger shuffle, protein-digestion feedback) all read Triglyceride, not Fat itself.

### Practical consequences for gameplay

- **Feeding fatty food = rapid Adipose accumulation.** A sustained diet of cheese, fat pills or meat pushes Fat → Triglyceride → Adipose (via reaction 10) much faster than a carb-only diet, because carb-only feeding has to run through the slow Pyruvate → FA → TG chain (three HL-621 reactions). Fat-food creatures fatten up noticeably faster.
- **A newborn has zero Fat but a huge Adipose reserve.** Because Fat [10] starts at 0 but Adipose [9] starts at amount 70 (~27 %), a newborn Norn begins life with no *incoming* fat but an enormous *stored* fat reserve. The creature can go a long time before it needs its first fatty meal.
- **One meal, one Fat pulse.** A single eat event spikes Fat once; the spike then decays exponentially at HL 116 as reaction 3 digests it. Testing a fatty feeder by repeatedly eating is a good way to inspect Fat's impulse response in the debug console (`CHEM 10 <amount>` to inject, then watch the trace).
- **Fat content of food matters as a food-item property.** Food authors choose the stim 78 dose their food writes per bite. Adjusting this value is the canonical way to tune "how fatty is this food" without touching the creature's biochemistry. Community food mods routinely expose this as the main tuning knob for nutritional content.
- **CAOS debugging.** `CHEM TARG 10` reads Fat concentration; `CHEM 10 <amount>` injects directly and will be digested by reaction 3 over the next ~minute into 3× Triglyceride + Cholesterol. This is a handy way to test Triglyceride/Cholesterol downstream effects without needing to author a food agent.
- **Fat cannot be destroyed or converted anywhere else.** There is no toxin (Geddonase, Cyanide, etc.) that consumes Fat, no emergency emitter that dumps Fat as a side product, and no decay. Reaction 3 is the only exit. This makes Fat one of the cleanest chemicals to reason about in the genome — its concentration is always exactly `∫(stim-78 inputs) − ∫(reaction-3 consumption)`.

### Summary of the Fat pipeline

```
  Food agent's eat script
       │
       │  STIM WRIT TARG 78 <dose>     (stim 78 → chem 10)
       ▼
  Fat (10)    [no decay, no receptors, no emitters]
       │
       │  reaction 3 (gene 25, HL 116, Medium)
       ▼
  3 × Triglyceride (8)  +  1 × Cholesterol (7)
       │                          │
       │                          └─► membranes, hormones, vitamin B pathway
       │
       ├─► reaction 10 (HL 621, 8 TG → 1 Adipose)       [long-term storage]
       └─► reaction 15 (HL 47, 1 TG → 3 Fatty Acid)     [rapid burn path]
```

Fat Tissue is therefore the **digestive-entry tier** of the creature's lipid economy: born at zero, topped up only by eating, silently invisible to every receptor in the body, and cleaved within roughly one minute of in-game time into the forms that actually fuel the creature. It is the first chemical a hungry Norn converts a meal into, and the first step of every "fat in the blood" story in the biochemistry.
