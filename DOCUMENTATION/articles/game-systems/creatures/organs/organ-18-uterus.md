# Organ #18 — Uterus

## Overview

Organ #18 is the creature's **pregnancy organ**. For most of a Norn's life it sits quietly in the background doing almost nothing — but when the creature becomes pregnant, it turns on and produces the single signal that shapes a pregnant Norn's mood: a steady trickle of **Comfort** that keeps her calm and content during gestation.

In real biology this corresponds to the **uterus / womb** — the organ in which gestation happens, whose activity is quiet outside of pregnancy and dominant during it.

---

## In-Game Role

### The comfort of being pregnant

When a female Norn is pregnant, Organ #18 continuously emits the **Comfort** chemical into the creature's bloodstream:

- Pregnant → Comfort

Comfort is one of the drive chemicals that tells the creature "things are okay right now". A pregnant Norn with a healthy Organ #18 therefore feels visibly **calmer, less agitated and more content** than she would without the signal — she is less prone to wandering aimlessly, less reactive to small stresses, and in general more settled. This is the in-game biochemistry behind the classic observation that pregnant Norns tend to be peaceful and a little more passive than their non-pregnant peers.

The emitter is gated strictly on the pregnant state: the moment a creature is not pregnant, the signal stops. Organ #18 does not produce Comfort "in general" — only while there is actually a gestation in progress.

### Quiet outside pregnancy

Outside of pregnancy, Organ #18 does nothing visible. It has no reactions to run and no other drives to emit. It keeps ticking at the same baseline clock as every other organ, paying its small ATP cost, participating in the creature's life-force / injury / repair system — but producing no observable effect.

In practice that means:

- For male creatures and non-pregnant females, Organ #18 is essentially dormant — the creature's behaviour is unaffected by whether it is in good condition or not.
- For a pregnant female, Organ #18 becomes one of the most important organs in her body, because it is **the only source** of the Comfort signal that defines the mood of her pregnancy.

A creature with a damaged or failed Organ #18 will therefore seem perfectly normal most of her life, but will have a **noticeably rougher pregnancy** — without Comfort flowing, she is likely to be more restless, more easily stressed by small annoyances, and generally less stable during gestation.

### Energy awareness

The organ watches the creature's current **Pyruvate** level — the body's general-purpose metabolic fuel. This lets it tune its behaviour to the mother's overall energy state: a pregnant Norn with plenty of fuel flowing through her body maintains a strong, steady Comfort signal, while one who is running low biochemically feels that dip echoed in her pregnancy mood.

It is a small effect, but it is another piece of the puzzle that makes a well-fed, well-kept pregnancy feel different from a stressed one — the Comfort does not arrive as a flat, environment-independent hormone, but as one subtly tied to the mother's current metabolic state.

---

## In-Game Effects Summary

- Emits a steady stream of **Comfort** while the creature is pregnant, keeping her visibly calm and content during gestation.
- Has no effect outside of pregnancy — the organ is dormant in males and in non-pregnant females.
- Gates its Comfort output on the mother's pyruvate level, so a pregnancy on a healthy, energetic body feels different from one in a depleted body.

In short: Organ #18 is the organ that makes being pregnant *feel* a particular way. Without it, gestation would happen silently in the background with none of its characteristic mood.

## Reactions, Receptors and Emitters

On the standard Starter Parent 1 genome, Organ #18 contains:

| Subsystem   | Count | Main role |
|-------------|-------|-----------|
| Reactions   | 0     | No chemical reactions — the organ is purely a sensor and emitter. |
| Receptors   | 1     | Reads the current Pyruvate level, tying Comfort output subtly to the mother's energy state. |
| Emitters    | 1     | Emits the Comfort chemical while the creature is pregnant. |
